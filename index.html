Where does the sun come from?

Your position

Click your position in the garden on the map. You can drag the marker afterwards.

The sun's path is shown for the 15th of the month, the hours of sun are averaged over the whole month.

How it works

From your position, a line is drawn towards the sun for every few minutes of the day. Where that line meets the garden outline is the part of the tree line the sun is above at that moment. If the sun is higher than the treetops seen from your position, the sun reaches you (yellow); otherwise you are in the shade of the trees (grey). The trees are treated as a continuous wall of the given height along the outline, with crowns reaching a few metres inwards.
