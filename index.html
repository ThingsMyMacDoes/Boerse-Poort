How it works

From the middle of your plot, a line is drawn towards the sun every few minutes of the day. Where that line meets the garden outline is shown in yellow, with hour marks: this is where you see the sun at that time. The outline of your plot is coloured by how much of the sun's energy comes from each direction: every moment is weighed by the height of the sun (a high sun shines through less air and hits the ground more steeply), from dark blue (no sun from that side) to red (where the strongest sun comes from). Clear-sky sun; buildings, trees and clouds are not taken into account.
